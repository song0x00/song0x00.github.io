// background

I'm a security researcher specialising in red team operations and wireless network security. Most of my time is split between physical and remote adversary simulation engagements, and finding new ways to abuse 802.11 in environments where defenders assume the air is safe.

Before going independent I spent several years on an internal red team, where I developed a habit of treating every engagement as a research project and writing up everything I couldn't find documented anywhere else. That's more or less why this blog exists.
