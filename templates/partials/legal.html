Approved targets only. Everything in this section is for systems you own, or systems you have explicit, written authorisation to test — with an agreed scope, timeframe and point of contact. If it isn't in scope, it isn't a target.

Unauthorised access, interception or interference is a criminal offence in most jurisdictions — in the UK under the Computer Misuse Act 1990, and for radio work the Wireless Telegraphy Act 2006 (deauthentication and jamming count as interference). Other countries have their own equivalents; know the ones that apply to you and your target.
